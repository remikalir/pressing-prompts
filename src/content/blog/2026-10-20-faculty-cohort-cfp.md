## Inviting Applications for the *Pressing Prompts* Duke Faculty Cohort: Critical Pedagogy and AI

Deadline: Monday, November 16, 2026, 5p ET

Apply: [Faculty Cohort Application](https://duke.qualtrics.com/jfe/form/SV_3yl53h3XhP95FSC)

Facilitators: Aria Chernik (CTL), Remi Kalir (CTL), Hannah Rozear (Libraries)

The [Duke Center for Teaching and Learning](https://ctl.duke.edu/) (CTL) and [Duke University Libraries](https://library.duke.edu/) invite faculty with urgent questions about AI and pedagogy to join a new faculty cohort rooted in critical inquiry, collaboration, and academic innovation.

Starting in January, faculty will collectively learn about, implement, and share insights about *Pressing Prompts* throughout the spring 2027 semester. Developed by Duke undergraduate students, faculty, and staff, *Pressing Prompts* is an openly-accessible educational resource that provides student-centered learning activities intended to guide curious and critical conversation about AI topics across multi-disciplinary classroom contexts. Unlike a static set of "best practices," *Pressing Prompts* is grounded in principles of critical pedagogy and educational experimentation, thereby enabling students and instructors to examine how AI raises crucial questions about our academic responsibilities, knowledge, labor, judgment, and learning.

## Cohort Participation Activities

- January: Kick-off meeting (in person) (90 minutes)
- February: Cohort members implement at least one *Pressing Prompts* conversation starter with students in their course (~15-30 minutes)
- March: Cohort members implement at least one in-class or online activity with students in their course (~1-2 class periods)
- April: Cohort members implement another conversation starter or activity with students (~15-60 minutes); facilitators gather feedback from students (~30 minutes)
- May: Cohort members participate in an online webinar to share their teaching experiments, student perspectives, and insights (60 minutes)
- May: Debrief meeting (online) (60 minutes)

With gratitude for participation, cohort members will receive $500 contributed to their research/discretionary account.

We invite applications from instructors of any rank teaching a class at Duke during spring semester 2027; instructors teaching at the undergraduate or graduate level and across all disciplines are welcome to [apply here](https://duke.qualtrics.com/jfe/form/SV_3yl53h3XhP95FSC).

Questions? Please contact Remi Kalir at [remi.kalir@duke.edu](mailto:remi.kalir@duke.edu).
